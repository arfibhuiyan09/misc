The Mathematics of 3D Raymarched Fractals

Unlike traditional 3D graphics rendered using polygon meshes (triangles), this application uses Raymarching and Signed Distance Fields (SDF) evaluated entirely inside a real-time GLSL Fragment Shader on your GPU.

1. Distance Estimation & Mandelbulb Formula

The Mandelbulb is a three-dimensional analogue of the Mandelbrot set constructed using spherical coordinates. For any point $\vec{p}$, we raise the vector to power $n$ using spherical transformations:

$v \to r^n \left( \sin(n\theta)\cos(n\phi), \sin(n\theta)\sin(n\phi), \cos(n\theta) \right) + c$

2. Sphere Tracing (Raymarching)

Instead of testing exact intersections, rays are marched step-by-step along their direction vector by the safe distance returned by the fractal's distance estimator function:

$d_i = \text{map}(ro + rd \cdot \sum d_k)$

3. Volumetric Accumulation & Glow

As the ray marches through space, energy is accumulated based on proximity to surfaces ($e^{-2|d|}$). This creates the signature neon atmospheric glow and multi-layered light scattering without pre-computed textures.
